1. Quick start

Five steps from nothing to your first recorded match.

  1. Sign up or log in. {{if .Aio}}Tap Sign up or Log in. You go to All-in-one's page at {{.Aio.Host}}, one account for Tally and other apps, and come back to Tally when you're done. Only enter your password on that address. Add your email too (see FAQ), so you can reset your password yourself. {{else}}Tap Sign up and pick a username and password.{{end}} Accounts are only for the admins who keep score. Players don't need one.
  2. Create a tally. On My tallies, enter a name (e.g. Sunday Smashers), check its link, and pick its type. The link and type can't be changed after creating the tally; the name and description can.
  3. Add player names. One name per line. You can paste a list straight from a WhatsApp group: numbers and bullets are removed, and names that already exist are skipped.
  4. Create a season. On the Seasons tab, set the start and end dates and choose the points rule.
  5. Start playing. Open the Play tab and pick the date, number of courts, and who's here. Continue in Play day.

3. Points rules

The points rule is set per season. Everyone on a side gets the same points. Since pairs are shuffled every match, points belong to individuals, not pairs.

Continuous score

One running score up to a target, e.g. 42; the second game carries on from the first. Each player gets their side's score.

A & B vs C & D, score 42–36 → A and B get 42 points each, C and D get 36 each.

2 games, score resets

Each game starts at 0, e.g. to 21. Points = the side's score across both games. Winning 1–1 counts as a draw. A single game can't end level.

21–9, 21–19 → A/B 42 points, C/D 28 points.
21–19, 18–21 → draw: A/B 39 points, C/D 40 points.

Win / draw / loss

Enter the final score; points follow the result (3 / 1 / 0 by default), not the size of the score. Equal scores are a draw.

Win bonus

Optional, for continuous and reset scores: the winning side gets extra points. A draw gets no bonus.

Accepted scores

Deuce scores (43–41, 30–29) and matches cut short when court time ran out (38–35) can still be recorded. Scores go up to 999, to catch typos.

Changing the rule mid-season

Once a season has matches, its score format is locked. The numbers (target, bonus, win/draw/loss points) can still change, and every point in the season is recalculated. Need another format? Start a new season.

4. Play day

Starting

On the Play tab, pick the date, the number of courts (1–3), and tick who's here. A date has only one play day; picking a date that already has one opens it. The date must fall inside a season.

{{template "gimg" (dict "P" $P "N" "start-day" "Alt" "The Start a play day form: date, courts, who's here")}}

Points or Matches

A play day has two tabs. Use either one, or both:

  • Points: enter each player's points like on a spreadsheet, without the pairs and opponents. Type a player's name (or pick it from the list), enter the points (0 up to the season's target, e.g. 42), then press Add. Each entry is one play, and a player can play at most 7 times a day. A name that isn't on the list is checked with you before it's added as a new player. In Today's points, tap a number to delete it, or + to add points for that player.
  • Matches: shuffle pairs, enter each match's score, and set who's here and the courts, as described below.

Points from both tabs go to the same leaderboard, and the tab you used last opens next time.

Shuffle pairs

Tap Shuffle pairs to fill every free court. So everyone gets a turn:

  1. whoever has played the least today goes first,
  2. then whoever has waited the longest,
  3. the rest is drawn at random. Partners and opponents are always random.

Below it you see who is waiting, in order, with how many matches they've played today (e.g. 2×).

{{template "gimg" (dict "P" $P "N" "courts" "Alt" "Two courts in play and the waiting list")}}

Changing players or cancelling

Someone has to leave, or wants to switch partners? Open Change players on the court card, pick someone else, and save it with the score. Cancel match removes an unfinished match; its players go back to the waiting list.

{{template "gimg" (dict "P" $P "N" "swap" "Alt" "A court card with Change players open")}}

Saving a score

Enter the score and tap Save score. Points are counted right away, the match moves to Finished, and the court is free again. Tap Shuffle pairs for the next turn.

Arriving late, leaving early

Open Attendance & courts at the bottom, tick or untick people, and save. You can change the number of courts there too. Late arrivals go first automatically, since they've played less.

Fixing mistakes and manual matches

In the Finished list, tap Edit to fix the players or score, or Delete. Points update with it. Use Add a match manually for matches whose pairs were arranged by hand.

{{template "gimg" (dict "P" $P "N" "done" "Alt" "The finished matches list with Edit and Delete")}}

Extra points

For bonuses or penalties outside matches (e.g. winning a tournament, arriving late). Enter points per player and a note; use a minus number for a penalty. Extra points count toward that play day.

{{template "gimg" (dict "P" $P "N" "awards" "Alt" "The Extra points form with a note")}}

Free counter tallies have no courts: the Record tab is simply the list of players to enter points for.

5. Leaderboard

The leaderboard is on the Overview tab, and anyone with the link can open it.

  • Order: total points. On equal points, whoever played fewer matches is higher, then by name. Every row keeps its own number.
  • Played = number of matches, Avg = points per match (wide screens only).
  • Arrows compare with the previous play day: ↑ up, ↓ down, ↔ same, and NEW for someone on the board for the first time.
  • As of: see the ranking as it was after a given week. Older seasons are under Season.
  • Below the board are that play day's match results.

Player page

Tap a name to see their rank, win–draw–loss record, points per play day, who they've partnered and played against, and all their matches.

6. Poster

Tap Poster on the leaderboard. The poster uses the season and play day currently selected, shows up to the top 20, and is 1024×1536 for WhatsApp groups or Instagram.

  • Download PNG saves the image.
  • Share (on phones that support it) opens the share menu, e.g. to WhatsApp.
{{template "gimg" (dict "P" $P "N" "poster" "Alt" "An example leaderboard poster")}}

Title, logo, and text

In Settings → Poster (or Edit title & logo on the poster page). All optional:

  • Title lines 1 and 2 (white and gold). If empty, the tally's name is split over two lines.
  • Logo: PNG, JPG, or WebP, up to 1 MB.
  • Text under the logo and footer text, e.g. Healthy together, fun together.

7. Managing a tally

Players

On the Players tab: Rename, Deactivate (no longer offered when picking players, but their points stay), or Delete. Delete only works for players who have never played or received points.

{{template "gimg" (dict "P" $P "N" "player-list" "Alt" "The player list with rename, deactivate, and delete")}}

New season

On the Seasons tab. Old seasons are kept and can be viewed from the Season picker on the leaderboard.

Other admins

In Settings → Admins, type their username. They have to {{if .Aio}}log in to Tally{{else}}sign up{{end}} once first. Admins can manage players, seasons, and matches; only the owner can add or remove admins.

Who can see it

Anyone with the link (the default) or admins only.

8. For players

Players don't need an account. Open the link from your admin to see the leaderboard, pick another play day, and tap your own name for your stats. Spotted a wrong score? Tell the admin; fixing it updates points and ranks right away.

9. FAQ

{{if .Aio}}
Forgot your password?

Tap Forgot password? on the home page. If your email is confirmed, a temporary password is sent there (it works for 30 minutes). Log in with it, then choose a new password. Your old password keeps working until then. No email yet? That page explains how to ask the admin for help.

How do I add an email or change my password?

Open the Account menu and tap Change password & email. The page is on {{.Aio.Host}} because your account is managed by All-in-one. After adding an email, click the confirmation link sent to it.

{{end}}
Why can't I delete a player?

They already have matches or points. Deactivate them instead: their name leaves the pickers, and the history stays correct.

I entered a wrong score. How do I fix it?

Open the play day (the Play tab, or Manage this play day on the leaderboard) and tap Edit in the Finished list.

Can I change the score format mid-season?

Not once there are matches. The win bonus and other numbers can still change, and points are recalculated. For another format, start a new season.

Someone arrived late. Will they still get to play?

Yes. Tick them in Attendance & courts. Since they've played less, they go first at the next shuffle.

The poster won't download on my iPhone.

Tap it again; iPhones sometimes need two tries. If it still fails, take a screenshot of the poster page.

Can the tally's link be changed?

No, so links you've already shared keep working. The name and description can be changed in Settings.

{{end}}
